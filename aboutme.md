👨 Mert Kosan, 1995 model.

I moved to [Istanbul](https://www.google.com/maps/place/%C4%B0stanbul,+Turkey/@41.0052363,28.8717536,11z/data=!3m1!4b1!4m5!3m4!1s0x14caa7040068086b:0xe1ccfe98bc01b0d0!8m2!3d41.0082376!4d28.9783589) for my undergraduate education. I had lived in Istanbul until I moved to [Santa Barbara](https://www.google.com/maps/place/Santa+Barbara,+CA/@34.398582,-119.7849608,13z/data=!3m1!4b1!4m5!3m4!1s0x80e914c76f2d83d5:0xc8d13a64d7ba7648!8m2!3d34.4208305!4d-119.6981901), CA for a Ph.D. I now live in Austin, TX.

👔 Working at Visa Inc. as a **Staff Machine Learning Scientist**. I design and build large-scale machine learning systems — self-supervised transformers, Large Transactional Models, and GenAI-powered explainability tools — for real-time fraud detection, scam detection, and risk modeling, protecting billions of transactions worldwide.

🎓 Obtained a **Ph.D. in Computer Science** from [University of California at Santa Barbara](https://www.ucsb.edu/), supervised by [Prof. Ambuj Singh](https://sites.cs.ucsb.edu/~ambuj/) at [Dynamo Lab](https://dynamo.cs.ucsb.edu/). My dissertation, *Transparent Representation Learning for Graphs and Human-AI Collaboration*, focused on explainability and representation learning for graph machine learning; my broader research interests also include anomaly detection. Two papers came out of this work: **GCFExplainer**, selected among the **Top 10 papers at WSDM 2023** and awarded **Best Paper at MLoG-WSDM 2023**, and **GNNX-Bench**, a comprehensive benchmarking study of graph neural network explainers published at **ICLR 2024**.

🎓 Obtained my **BSc in Computer Science** from Sabanci University, Turkey. I worked on **Graph based Recommendation Systems**, supervised by [Prof. Yucel Saygin](http://people.sabanciuniv.edu/ysaygin/), for my bachelor graduation project. I **ranked 1<sup>st</sup>** in Computer Science department.

📄 My work has been published at **KDD**, **ICLR**, **AAAI**, and **WSDM**, with 250+ citations and an h-index of 7. I hold **three patents** in machine learning for fraud detection and anomaly analysis, and I serve as an **Area Chair** for KDD's Dataset and Benchmark Track.

🧠 **Result oriented**, **responsible**, and **hardworking** are three adjectives that describe how I work. I pick things up quickly, and I've picked up a fair amount of teaching experience along the way — I enjoy mentoring junior scientists and engineers just as much as I enjoy the research itself.
